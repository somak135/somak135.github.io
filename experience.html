Internship
Jun – Jul 2021

Capital One Datalabs

Data Analyst Intern · Bangalore, India

Two months on the Data Science team, spent building a Bayesian A/B testing framework. Monte Carlo simulation drove both halves of it: choosing sample sizes for the control and treatment groups before a test is run, and evaluating the result afterwards. A Bayesian test reaches the efficiency of a two-sample t-test on substantially smaller samples, so experiments become cheaper, and at a company running many of them that difference compounds.

The deliverable was an interactive widget in a Jupyter notebook, so that colleagues without a statistics background could set up a test and read its result themselves rather than routing every experiment through an analyst. The internship ended with a pre-placement offer.
