About

I work on random graphs, graph neural networks, high-dimensional statistics, and most recently on multi-agent LLM systems. I am advised by Morgane Austern. I am looking for a quantitative research internship for summer 2027.

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Statistics at Harvard University. Before Harvard I completed my B.Stat. and M.Stat. at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, and then spent a year as a data scientist at Capital One Datalabs in Bangalore, India.

At the moment I am working on a few different things. One is over-parameterized regression, and what the pattern of zeros in a design matrix does to its risk curve. Another is graph attention, and whether it can be proved to beat plain neighbourhood averaging instead of merely observed to win on benchmarks. Then there is a random graph model where arriving vertices are blocked from attaching to the biggest hubs, which I have been at since my master's. The newest is about populations of language model agents settling on a convention none of them picked individually.
